# Workshop Details

The workshop consisted of a series of talks and practical sessions delivered at the University of York over the course of 2 days (23-24 April). It covered setting up both EPOCH and sdf-xarray along with delving into the details of how PIC codes work. The timetable is located [here](https://epochpic.github.io/quickstart/epoch_workshop_2026.html) and recordings of the presentations can be found on [YouTube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLXfw-OhAIheRtwRqrGZvMqFuQ2GrIBLVl&si=iDbh0h-XYEUcfoDM).
