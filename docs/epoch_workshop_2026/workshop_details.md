# Workshop Details

The workshop consists of a series of talks and practical sessions delivered at the University of York over the course of 2 days (23-24 April). It covers setting up both EPOCH and sdf-xarray along with delving into the details of how PIC codes work. The timetable can be found here <https://epochpic.github.io/quickstart/epoch_workshop_2026.html>.
